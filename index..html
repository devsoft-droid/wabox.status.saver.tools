Privacy Policy — WA Box: Status Saver & Tools

Last updated: September 30, 2026 · Package: com.wabox.status.saver.tools

WA Box is an independent utility app. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp, Meta, or any messaging company.

What the app does

  • Direct Chat by phone number
  • On-device Audio to Text
  • Fancy Unicode fonts
  • Status Saver (copies you choose to save)
  • Optional local Message Recovery from notifications

Data

WA Box is designed to work on your device. We do not require an account and we do not operate a WA Box backend that stores your chats.

We do not sell personal data.

Permissions

  • Microphone — speech to text
  • Notification access (optional) — recovery
  • Storage / media / folder access — statuses
  • Internet — messenger links

Retention

Local data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall WA Box. Uninstalling removes WA Box’s local data.

Children

WA Box is not directed at children under 13.
